Goal colours & app guide

Red means below 50%, yellow at least 50% but below 100%, and green at least 100% of your chosen time goal. Unrounded times determine the colour. Colours do not indicate medical treatment success.

With a daily goal, past days and today are compared with that daily target. Zero minutes is below 50%. Today’s result is provisional.

A weekly goal covers Monday to Sunday. The coloured weekly row includes the full week, even across month boundaries. Individual days remain neutral and show their minutes. The current week is still in progress.

Without a goal the calendar and ring stay neutral. Future days and weeks are not rated.

All periods are compared with your current goal. Changing your goal also changes colours for earlier periods.
