Find your training rhythm

  1. Open settings

    Go to Help & Information → Reminders & iCloud. Three reminders are available, each with its own on/off switch.

  2. Choose times and days

    Enable the reminders you want. Select a time and weekdays for each, such as weekday mornings and weekend evenings. A reminder with no selected weekdays produces no notification.

  3. Save and allow

    Tap Save reminders and allow notifications when iOS asks. If permission was denied, open notification settings. Times follow the device’s local time zone.

  4. Consider other devices

    When sync is enabled, reminder times sync but notification permission stays specific to each device. To receive reminders on only one device, disable app notifications in iOS on the other. A reminder does not start training automatically.

Try it yourself

Set one reminder a few minutes ahead on today’s weekday. Save, allow notifications and check the reminder. Afterwards, restore your preferred everyday times.

What success looks like

The reminder appears at the test time if iOS settings allow it. Otherwise check the weekday, permission and Focus, then reopen PhysioClock before another test.

Synchronisation and device activity

Always

Local data and iCloud are compared when opening the app and returning to the foreground. During use, changes sync automatically, at the latest during the next periodic sync.

Manual

Training data is sent and loaded only with “Sync now”. Local changes stay on this device until then. Activity checks for the startup warning still run automatically. This choice applies only to this device.

“Active” means the app is in the foreground and recently reported through iCloud. Status refreshes about every 45 seconds and expires after 150 seconds without a heartbeat. Internet, the same Apple Account, enabled sync and this feature on both devices are required. The warning does not lock devices.
