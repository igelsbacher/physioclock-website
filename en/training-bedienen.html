Control your training

The entry page shows your progress ring, training goal and wellbeing. “Continue to training” opens the timer and exercise controls. Only “Start” begins training. “Back to overview” returns to the entry page; an active session continues running.

← Back to overview

  1. Start and read the display

    Load your prepared set and tap Start. The large countdown shows the time left for the current exercise. Separate progress indicators show the exercise and the whole session.

  2. Pause and skip

    Pause stops the session temporarily; Resume continues it. Next skips the current exercise. Skipped time does not count as active training. Reset discards an unfinished session.

  3. Finish and review

    End finishes a started session and saves it as ended early if it is not complete. A normally completed session is also recorded. Open the monthly view using the calendar icon.

  4. Signals and screen

    Configure sound and vibration in the exercise editor. iOS notifications, silent mode and Focus can affect signals. On iPhone, landscape view is available when rotation is unlocked. Check signals before first use.

Try it yourself

Use a short test set: Start → Pause → Resume → End. Check the calendar afterwards. Delete the test record from history if you do not want to keep it.

What success looks like

The countdown stops while paused. After End, the started session appears in history; only active time counts.

Next module →

Optional exercise

Mark optional exercises in the exercise editor. They follow the required part. Choose whether to start them or finish for today. Declining still completes the required part. Decision time does not count as training. Both progress bars have midpoint ticks and a two-second “Halfway” cue. The countdown turns white during the final 15 seconds of each exercise.

Set volume

100% is the standard signal. Adjust its level from −50% to +50%. System volume stays unchanged; Silent mode, Focus and iOS notification volume still apply.

Goal colours & app guide

Red means below 50%, yellow at least 50% but below 100%, and green at least 100% of your chosen time goal. Unrounded times determine the colour. Colours do not indicate medical treatment success.

Synchronisation and device activity

Always

Local data and iCloud are compared when opening the app and returning to the foreground. During use, changes sync automatically, at the latest during the next periodic sync.

Manual

Training data is sent and loaded only with “Sync now”. Local changes stay on this device until then. Activity checks for the startup warning still run automatically. This choice applies only to this device.

“Active” means the app is in the foreground and recently reported through iCloud. Status refreshes about every 45 seconds and expires after 150 seconds without a heartbeat. Internet, the same Apple Account, enabled sync and this feature on both devices are required. The warning does not lock devices.

Display and training controls

The current time and finish time have the same size. On iPad, the next element is also shown in large type when “Show the next training step” is enabled. Simple mode keeps the display reduced.

While training is running, Back returns to the beginning of the previous element, including a scheduled rest. It is disabled at the first element and hidden when paused or finished. Only active time actually repeated is added; jumping back adds no time.

In Help → Settings, you can enable speech with PhysioClock Plus. It reads the element text at its beginning, including side and round; halfway and completion are also announced. Resuming alone does not repeat the text. Foreground only, with no recording or microphone access. iOS and audio settings may affect announcements.

Halfway through each running element, “Halfway” fills the screen for two seconds. Training continues. Both progress bars keep their 50% markers.

When the program finishes, a distinct ascending chime plays if sound is enabled. After optional exercises or declining them, “Warm up for longer?” appears. Yes starts the selected extension of the last element; No closes the prompt. Decision time does not count. The completed part stays saved; ending the extension updates the same history entry. Choosing End does not offer an extension.

You can extend the last training element once. Choose 60, 120, 180 or 240 seconds and confirm with Yes. Your saved set stays unchanged.

Whenever the welcome page opens, movement symbols cycle for about five seconds. The iOS Reduce Motion setting disables the cycling.

Add a meditative cool-down?

Use the selected 60, 120, 180 or 240 seconds for an optional quiet break. The original instrumental music works offline. This time does not count as active training. No exercise, breathing or therapy instructions.

At the end, choose a meditative cool-down directly instead of extending training. After an accepted training extension, the cool-down is offered separately again, with the same duration preselected. It starts only after confirmation. You can switch Meditation music off before starting or during the phase. The original, quiet instrumental soundscape plays for the chosen duration and then stops. System volume and silent mode still apply; the short training-signal switch does not control this explicitly selected music. Pause and End stop the music. Leaving the app, an audio interruption or disconnecting headphones pauses the phase; Resume starts it again. Your existing history entry and active training minutes remain unchanged.

A calmer, more reliable experience

After reopening, resume or discard an interrupted session. A local checkpoint is saved about every five seconds and at pause, navigation and app-background transitions. The last seconds may be missing after an abrupt exit. Only captured time and active segments are restored; time since saving does not count. A paused session stays paused. Pending optional-exercise or cool-down choices remain choices and start nothing automatically. Training-data sync is blocked while recovery is undecided or a restored session is active.

The compact summary shows recorded active training time, movement elements run to their end and progress towards your current personal time goal. Skipped elements are not completed. A repeated element counts at most once as completed; its repeated active time still counts. Cool-down appears separately and adds no training minutes. This is a time and control summary, not an assessment of movement quality or health outcomes.

Meditation music fades gently during the final five seconds and stops with the timer. The selected duration and music on/off choice are remembered on this device for the next offer. Even after restarting, cool-down begins only after confirmation.

Removing an exercise in either editor shows Undo for eight seconds. It restores the last removed exercise with its details near its former position. With multiple removals, Undo applies to the last one. Saving or closing the editor ends the offer.

The eye control in the overview and Help → Settings hide wellbeing, steps, personal goals and their history views, including from VoiceOver. Goal progress in the summary is hidden too. The goal-widget cache is cleared; iOS controls when an already visible widget refreshes. This is a local display option, not an access lock or deletion. Existing data, explicitly requested exports and sync remain available. Training name, timer and active duration remain visible.
