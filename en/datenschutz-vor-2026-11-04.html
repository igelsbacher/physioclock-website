Privacy

Training data is stored locally. If you enable iCloud sync, sets, custom exercises, history, training settings and reminder times are also stored with Apple in your Apple Account’s private CloudKit database. No such sync occurs unless enabled. Notification permission is specific to each device. Disabling sync does not delete existing cloud data. Cloud data can be removed through iCloud storage management; the interface depends on your system version. No separate PhysioClock account, advertising or location access. Keep exports secure. GitHub Pages may process connection data when you visit this website. Contact: iphysioclock@gmail.com.

Training goals

Your goal also syncs when iCloud sync is enabled. JSON export includes the goal; import still creates a new set and does not fully restore goals or history. Removing a goal keeps training history. Deleting training records reduces calculated goal progress.

Wellbeing & history

Entries and notes are stored locally. With iCloud enabled, they also sync in your Apple Account’s private database. JSON export includes these entries. The current importer creates a new training set and does not restore wellbeing history. Delete all training data also removes wellbeing entries and notes.

Steps today

Shows steps available in Apple Health since midnight in your local time zone. Updates when opened and about every minute. Steps do not change training minutes or the goal ring.

PhysioClock reads steps only and writes no Health data. The step count is displayed temporarily and is not stored, exported or transferred through PhysioClock iCloud sync.

Access is optional. A dash replaces zero when no readable data is available. Health does not let the app distinguish denied read access from missing data. Manage permissions in Apple Health.

Voluntary feedback

Feedback links open an editable email draft. In the app it includes the app version, build, iOS version and device family, but no training or health data. Support receives your message and sender address only when you send the email yourself. Please do not include health data.

Widget and Lock Screen

Weekly plan, favourites and recently used sets are stored locally and also synced when iCloud sync is enabled. Display, speech and widget choices are local to each device. If widget details are enabled, only training minutes, time goal and update time are saved in a local shared app container for the extension. Disabling removes this cache. A Live Activity receives only total remaining time and status, with no exercise names, wellbeing or notes. It may be visible on the Lock Screen. The trial is not saved.
