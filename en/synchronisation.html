Synchronisation and device activity

Always

Local data and iCloud are compared when opening the app and returning to the foreground. During use, changes sync automatically, at the latest during the next periodic sync.

Manual

Training data is sent and loaded only with “Sync now”. Local changes stay on this device until then. Activity checks for the startup warning still run automatically. This choice applies only to this device.

“Active” means the app is in the foreground and recently reported through iCloud. Status refreshes about every 45 seconds and expires after 150 seconds without a heartbeat. Internet, the same Apple Account, enabled sync and this feature on both devices are required. The warning does not lock devices.

PhysioClock is active on another device

Another device recently reported PhysioClock in the foreground. Avoid editing the same entries simultaneously. “Continue” keeps the app usable; in “Always”, data sync then starts, while “Manual” waits for “Sync now”.

When iCloud sync is enabled, PhysioClock reports foreground status using a random installation identifier and device kind in your private iCloud database. This enables startup warnings on your other device, including in manual mode. Activity reports contain neither your personal device name nor training content. “Continue” closes this one-time information notice; alternatively, turn sync off. Details are under Help → Privacy & acknowledgements.
