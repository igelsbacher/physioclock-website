Privacy & acknowledgements

In the app under Help → Privacy & acknowledgements, find current notices and acknowledgements recorded on each device. Saved text versions remain readable offline in the app. This website does not store personal acknowledgements.

The JSON export contains times, language, versions and text copies, not training or wellbeing data. It is saved only through your file selection. Keep your own copy. The log is separate from training backups and app iCloud sync and excluded from automatic device backups. App deletion or device loss can remove it.

The older app stored only an acknowledgement and possibly a device time. Its text version, language, time zone and app version are unknown. This entry is not a new acknowledgement of the current texts.

This acknowledgement does not enable Apple Health, iCloud or other optional permissions.

Times come from the device clock. This local log is not independent proof of identity or actual time. The text checksum detects changed text; it is not a signature or external certification.
