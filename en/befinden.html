Example entries

A check-in that suits you

Open Record wellbeing on the main screen. Choose one of five labelled options; none is preselected. Add an optional note of up to 300 characters and adjust the date and time. Save creates the entry; Later closes the form without saving.

After training or at any time

After a saved session, the main screen offers a check-in card. There is no mandatory pop-up. You can skip it or add an entry manually without training. Post-training entries can be linked to a session.

Chart, calendar and list

In Wellbeing & history, choose 7, 30 or 90 days. The chart and calendar show the last entry of each day. Days without entries have no point. Lines only connect existing entries. Select a day in the monthly calendar to list all entries from that day.

Edit and delete

Use an entry’s menu to edit its time, choice or note, or delete it after confirmation. The display recalculates. Deleting a training session does not automatically remove a separately saved wellbeing entry.

Personal data

Entries and notes are stored locally. With iCloud enabled, they also sync in your Apple Account’s private database. JSON export includes these entries. The current importer creates a new training set and does not restore wellbeing history. Delete all training data also removes wellbeing entries and notes.

Not a measure of training effectiveness

These entries are subjective snapshots. They change neither active time nor the goal ring. They cannot demonstrate a medical effect of training. The five categories are not combined into a clinical score or outcome prediction.

Steps today

Shows steps available in Apple Health since midnight in your local time zone. Updates when opened and about every minute. Steps do not change training minutes or the goal ring.

PhysioClock reads steps only and writes no Health data. The step count is displayed temporarily and is not stored, exported or transferred through PhysioClock iCloud sync.

Access is optional. A dash replaces zero when no readable data is available. Health does not let the app distinguish denied read access from missing data. Manage permissions in Apple Health.
