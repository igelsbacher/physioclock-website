Privacy

Your exercise programmes, custom exercises, sets and session history are stored locally in the app. No account is required. The app contains no advertising and does not request location access. You may manually export or import your data in JSON format; keep exported files secure. When you visit this website, GitHub Pages may process technical connection data. External websites have their own privacy policies. Privacy enquiries: iphysioclock@gmail.com. These statements describe the current project and must be checked against the final app before App Store publication.
