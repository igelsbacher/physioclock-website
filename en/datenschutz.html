Privacy

Training data is stored locally. If you enable iCloud sync, sets, custom exercises, history, training settings and reminder times are also stored with Apple in your Apple Account’s private CloudKit database. No such sync occurs unless enabled. Notification permission is specific to each device. Disabling sync does not delete existing cloud data. Cloud data can be removed through iCloud storage management; the interface depends on your system version. No separate PhysioClock account, advertising or location access. Keep exports secure. GitHub Pages may process connection data when you visit this website. Contact: iphysioclock@gmail.com.
