Get started – step by step

Take your time. You do not need to learn every feature at once.

1. Choose simple mode

Choose Simple mode when you first open the app. New users then see the separate usage notice. Choosing a mode does not grant any optional permissions.

2. Choose a programme

Tap Choose a programme. Load one of your saved sets or open Templates A, B and C. Choose duration, body focus and exercise count. Your current exercise plan changes only when you apply the template.

3. Adjust exercises if needed

In Edit exercises, change names and durations, add exercises or remove them. Save under a new name creates your own copy. Enter a name that is not already in use, then Save. Cancelling the name dialog keeps your draft in the editor.

4. Open training and start

Open training shows the timer. Timing begins only when you press Start. The current exercise and remaining time appear in large text.

5. Pause, Next and End

Pause stops the timer; Resume continues it. Next skips the current exercise. End finishes the session after confirmation and records actual movement time. Optional exercises are offered at the end.

6. Find it again

Your saved copy is under Choose a programme. End a running or paused session before switching programmes or editing exercises.

Further settings

Help → Settings → Display mode opens the mode choice. High contrast stays active in simple mode; you can increase text size further. Larger iOS accessibility text is respected. Existing rounds, rests, side changes, optional exercises and signals are kept when switching.

On this website, use your browser’s zoom to enlarge text. All steps remain understandable without animations.
