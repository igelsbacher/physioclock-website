Understanding movement

Regular movement supports health and wellbeing. Short active breaks count too. Start at a level that suits your abilities and build up gradually.

For persistent nonspecific low back pain, an individually tailored exercise programme may help. No single form of exercise is best for everyone. Physical, psychological and social factors can influence pain.

As general guidance, WHO recommends 150–300 minutes of moderate or 75–150 minutes of vigorous activity per week for adults, plus muscle strengthening on at least two days. This is not an individual therapy prescription.

PhysioClock supports planning and recording. It does not replace a medical assessment or personal treatment advice. Seek medical advice for new, persistent or worsening symptoms.

The ring shows progress towards your time goal, not medical treatment success. Wellbeing records are personal reports and do not establish an effect of training.
